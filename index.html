We are: - Live Stage: A platform for artists who wants to play live instrumental music or using a DJ equipment. - Art Space: An artistic corner equipped with easels and paints. All you need to bring is a canvas and express yourself to the fullest. With today’s abundance of impressions, there’s no shortage of inspiration. - Rehearsals/Recording: Come in any time before the venue opens to rehearse your material. If your material is already polished, you can record it with us alongside professional sound engineers who will ensure your work sounds top-quality. - Bookstore: A carefully curated collection of exceptional books selected by an our friend philosopher. You can even discuss them with the him right there. - Beverage atelier: Enjoy our signature cocktails and infusions crafted entirely by crew’s hands. - Video Production: For those who have recorded their music and want to document their performance with multi-camera setups. You can also shoot a music video, but only if we like the concept. We’ll assist you with pre-production planning and potentially even post-production (though that’s not guaranteed). - Tea Room: A cozy spot where you can enjoy a vast variety of teas from different parts of Asia. All these opportunities are available every day except Mondays. Come visit us; we’ll be happy to see you! Your involvement is what allows us to continue doing what we love.

You can support us with any donation. We’ll be immensely grateful, and every time we release music or video projects, we’ll acknowledge you as someone who helped us.
